Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Yobi ("we," "our," or "us") is a personal budgeting app built around the Kakeibo method. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Yobi is operated by Robert Seligmann, who acts as the data controller for the limited personal data described below. By using the Yobi app or visiting yobi.app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how people use the app. This includes:

  • Events and interactions (e.g., which features you use, screens you visit)
  • App version, device type, and operating system version
  • Session duration and frequency of use
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level)

Analytics events are linked to a random device identifier rather than your identity or any financial data, and are used mainly to understand where people drop off during onboarding so we can improve it. Yobi does not include an in-app toggle to disable analytics; you can limit what your device shares through your iOS privacy settings, and you can email us to request deletion of the analytics associated with your device (see "Your Rights").

Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes or encounters an error, Apple (iOS) may collect crash logs and device diagnostics through its standard platform reporting. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Waitlist email. If you sign up for the Yobi waitlist on our marketing website, your email address is collected and stored by Tally, the form provider we use. We use this only to contact you about Yobi's launch and updates. We do not share your email with third parties for marketing purposes.

Exchange rates. To display live multi-currency conversions, the app fetches current exchange rates from a third-party rate provider. These requests carry only standard technical metadata (such as your IP address) — never your balances, transactions, or any financial data you have entered.

Your financial data stays on your device. All budgets, transactions, and entries you create in Yobi are stored locally on your device only. Yobi has no servers of its own, so this data is never sent to us — or to any third party. The only network requests the app makes are to fetch exchange rates and to send anonymous usage analytics, and neither includes your balances or transactions.

Security. Because your financial data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device's own security — passcode, biometrics, and operating-system encryption. For the limited information we do handle (your waitlist email and anonymous analytics), we rely on established providers — Tally and PostHog — that maintain industry-standard security practices.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To understand which features are useful and improve the app
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and bugs
  • To communicate Yobi launch news to waitlist subscribers (email only)

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Legal basis (EU/UK). Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent to contact you through the waitlist (which you may withdraw at any time) and on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app to process anonymous analytics and crash data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time — see "Your Rights" below.

3. Third-Party Services

Yobi uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • PostHog — product analytics. See posthog.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store — app distribution and crash reporting. See apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Exchange-rate provider — supplies live currency conversion rates used for multi-currency totals.
  • Tally — waitlist form. See tally.so/help/privacy-policy

On our marketing website (yobi.app) we embed Tally's waitlist form and load fonts from Google Fonts. Both are served by those third parties and may receive your IP address as part of delivering their content. The website itself does not run product analytics.

4. Data Retention

Analytics data collected via PostHog is retained in accordance with PostHog's standard data retention policies. Crash data is retained per Apple's platform policies. Waitlist emails are retained until you request removal or the waitlist is closed.

Because your financial data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes all of that data.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data. These include:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request that we delete your data
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to our processing of your data
  • Opt-out of sale — we do not sell personal data, so this right is not applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at robert.seligmann@pm.me. We will respond within 30 days. EU/UK residents may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Children's Privacy

Yobi is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. International Data Transfers

PostHog may process analytics data outside your country of residence. PostHog is a participant in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and provides appropriate safeguards for data transfers from the EEA and UK. See PostHog's privacy policy for details.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of Yobi after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
